Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for the Non-Medalist championship?▼
Only swimmers who have never won an individual medal (Gold, Silver, Bronze) at State, Zonal, National, or major School Games (CBSE/SGFI) are eligible. Relay medals do not affect eligibility.
What documents must we submit on Day 1?▼
Signed Non-Medalist Declaration (Coach + Parent), institutional representation certificate, and government age-proof document (Birth Certificate, Aadhaar, or School ID).
How does the One Start Rule work?▼
Any swimmer who leaves the block before the starting signal is immediately disqualified (DSQ). The race continues for all other swimmers without a recall.
Is there a refund policy?▼
Fees are non-refundable once paid, except in case of official event cancellation by the organiser. No event swaps after the registration deadline.
Can a swimmer represent both school and academy?▼
No. Under SFI guidelines, a swimmer must declare single representation for the meet — either school OR academy, not both.

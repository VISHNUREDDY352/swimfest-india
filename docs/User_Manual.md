## Part 1: Swimmer Guide

---

### 1.1 Getting Started

**Website URL:** https://swimfest-india.onrender.com  
**Local URL:** http://localhost:5000

When you open the website, you will see the **Login Page**. You must create an account before you can register for any event.

---

### 1.2 Creating an Account

1. On the Login page, click **"Create Account"** (bottom of the form)
2. Fill in the registration form:

**Swimmer Details**
- Full Name (official name as on birth certificate)
- Gender (Boy / Girl)
- Date of Birth (must be between 01/01/2010 and 31/12/2017)
- Address

**School Details** *(optional if Academy is filled)*
- School Name
- Board (CBSE / ICSE / State Board / International)
- Class / Grade

**Parent / Guardian Details**
- Parent Name
- Relationship (Father / Mother / Guardian)
- Parent Mobile (used for login)
- Email Address (used for login + confirmation)

**Academy** *(optional if School is filled)*
- Select your swimming academy from the dropdown

**Password**
- Create a password (min 6 characters)
- Confirm password

3. Click **"Create Account"**
4. You will be redirected to the Login page

> ℹ️ You must fill either School Details OR Academy — at least one is required.

---

### 1.3 Logging In

1. Open the website → Login page appears
2. Select the **"Swimmer"** tab (default)
3. Enter your **Email Address** or **Parent Mobile Number**
4. Enter your **Password**
5. Click **"Sign In as Swimmer"**
6. You will be redirected to the **Home Page**

---

### 1.4 Home Page

After login, you can see:
- **Upcoming Events** — all open tournaments sorted by registration deadline
- **Registration Closes In** — countdown timer for the soonest deadline
- **Eligibility Checker** — enter your DOB to check which events you qualify for
- **Swimming Tips** — race day preparation tips
- **How It Works** — 4-step guide to registration
- **Announcements** — important notices
- **Quick Register** — fast registration shortcut
- **Swimmer Directory** — registered swimmers
- **Age Categories & Events** — full event list per category
- **FAQ** — frequently asked questions
- **Contact** — venue and contact information

---

### 1.5 Checking Eligibility

Before registering, check if you are eligible:

1. On the Home page, find the **"Eligibility Checker"** card
2. Enter the swimmer's **Date of Birth**
3. Select **Gender**
4. The system will show your **Age Group** and eligible events
5. If DOB is outside the range (2010–2017), you are not eligible

**Age Groups:**
- U-10: Born 2016 or 2017
- U-12: Born 2014 or 2015
- U-14: Born 2012 or 2013
- U-16: Born 2010 or 2011

---

### 1.6 Registering for an Event (7-Step Process)

Click **"Register"** on any open event card, or use **Quick Register**.

#### Step 1 — Select Tournament
- Choose the tournament you want to enter
- Only open tournaments with active registration deadlines are shown
- Click **"Book Tournament"** to proceed

#### Step 2 — Player Profile
- Your saved profile details auto-fill the form
- Review and update if needed:
  - Swimmer Name, Gender, Date of Birth
  - Parent details (name, mobile, email)
  - School or Academy
- Click **"Next"**

#### Step 3 — Select Events
- Choose up to **3 individual events**
- You may also opt in for **1 relay event**
- Events shown are based on your age group
- Click **"Next"**

#### Step 4 — Seed Times
- Enter your best time for each selected event
- Format: MM:SS.ss (e.g. 00:45.32)
- If you don't have a seed time, select "NT" (No Time)
- Click **"Next"**

#### Step 5 — Review & Declaration
- Review all your details, selected events, and fees
- Read and tick all 5 consent boxes:
  - Tournament Rules
  - Refund Policy
  - Parent Consent
  - Medical Consent
  - Non-Medalist Declaration
- Click **"Proceed to Payment"**

#### Step 6 — Payment
- A Razorpay payment popup opens
- Pay using:
  - UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  - Debit/Credit Card
  - Net Banking
- Test card: 4111 1111 1111 1111, any expiry, any CVV
- After successful payment, you proceed to Step 7

#### Step 7 — Confirmation
- Your booking is confirmed
- Booking ID is shown
- Confirmation email is sent to your parent's email
- WhatsApp message is prepared for your parent's number
- Click **"View Booking"** to see your registration details

---

### 1.7 My Profile Page

Click **"My Profile"** in the navigation bar.

**You can see:**
- Profile photo, name, swimmer ID, age group
- Personal Details (name, gender, DOB, nationality, address)
- Parent/Guardian details
- School details
- Academy details
- Current Booking (registered events, seed times, payment status)
- My Event Entries (heat/lane/final time after event)
- Competition History
- Required Documents list
- Participation Certificate (available only after event is marked Completed)

**To Edit Profile:**
1. Click **"Edit Profile"** button
2. Update any field (name, DOB, address, school, academy, parent details)
3. Upload profile photo (JPG/PNG, max 2MB)
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

---

### 1.8 Participation Certificate

- The certificate is only available **after** the admin marks the tournament as "Completed"
- Go to My Profile → scroll to find the **"Participation Certificate"** card
- Click **"Download Certificate (PDF)"**
- The certificate shows your name, age group, events and tournament details

---

### 1.9 Logging Out

- Click **"Logout"** in the navigation bar (top right)
- On mobile: tap the ☰ menu → scroll down → tap "Logout"
- You will be redirected to the Login page

---

### 1.10 Documents to Bring on Race Day

You must bring **originals** of the following on Day 1:

1. **Birth Certificate** (government issued)
2. **Aadhaar Card** (or equivalent age proof)
3. **Non-Medalist Declaration** (signed by Coach + Parent)
4. **Parent Consent Form** (signed)

> ⚠️ Incomplete documents may result in disqualification.

### 2.3 Managing Events (Tournaments)

Click **"Events"** in the admin nav bar.

#### Create a New Event
Fill in the form at the top:
- Event Name
- Venue (full address — used for Google Maps)
- Start Date and End Date
- Registration Deadline
- Fee per Event (INR)
- Number of Lanes (6 / 8 / 10)
- Status (Open / Upcoming)

Click **"Create Event"**

#### Edit an Event
- Click **"Edit"** button on any event row
- Update name, venue, dates, fee, or lanes
- Click **"Save Changes"**

#### Change Event Status
- Use the **dropdown** on each event row to change status:
  - **Open** — registration is active (appears on home page)
  - **Upcoming** — not yet open for registration
  - **Ongoing** — event is currently happening
  - **Closed** — registration closed, event not started
  - **Completed** — event finished (triggers certificate availability)

> ℹ️ Changing status updates the home page automatically when users switch to that tab.

---

### 2.4 Managing Swimmers

Click **"Swimmers"** in the admin nav bar.

- View all registered swimmers with their details
- Click **"Edit"** to update a swimmer's profile (name, gender, DOB, mobile, email)

---

### 2.5 Managing Bookings

Click **"Bookings"** in the admin nav bar.

- View all bookings with swimmer name, tournament, amount and status
- Each booking shows booking ID, swimmer ID, tournament and payment status

---

### 2.6 Generating Heat Sheets

Click **"Heat Sheet"** in the admin nav bar.

1. Select the **Tournament** from the dropdown
2. Select the number of **Lanes** (6, 8, or 10)
3. Optionally filter by age group or gender
4. Click **"Generate Heat Sheet"**
5. The system automatically:
   - Groups swimmers by event/age/gender
   - Sorts by seed time (fastest to slowest)
   - Assigns lanes (fastest swimmers get centre lanes)
   - Calculates start times (3 min/heat)
6. View the generated heat sheet on screen
7. Export as **CSV** or **PDF** using the buttons at top

---

### 2.7 Uploading Results

Click **"Results Upload"** button in the admin nav bar.

1. Select the tournament from the dropdown
2. The list of all swimmer entries appears
3. For each swimmer, enter:
   - **Final Time** (format: MM:SS.ss)
   - **Overall Place** (#1, #2, etc.)
4. Click **"Save"** for each entry

> After uploading results, mark the tournament as "Completed" to enable certificate downloads for participants.

---

### 2.8 Managing Academies

Click **"Academies"** in the admin nav bar.

- View all registered academies
- Add new academy:
  - Academy Name, Short Name
  - Head Coach, Mobile, Email
  - City, Address
  - Click "Add Academy"

---

### 2.9 Logging Out

Click **"Logout"** (red button) in the admin panel header.
